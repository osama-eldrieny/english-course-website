About the Course

You know English. Now let's get you speaking it.

You understand the grammar and you know the vocabulary. Maybe you can read and write perfectly well — then someone speaks to you in English and suddenly you're translating, searching for words or planning the entire sentence before you say it. Well, this course is built for that gap.

Our Speaking & Fluency program helps you move from knowing English to actually using it.

Through discussions, simulations, problem-solving, presentations, interactive challenges, debates and real-life situations, you'll practise responding more spontaneously and expressing yourself more naturally.

You'll also work on:

Fluency · Pronunciation · Vocabulary · Natural Expression · Listening & Responding · Confidence

Because Speakademi classes bring together learners from different backgrounds, you'll practise communicating with people who don't necessarily share your first language. That's where English becomes real.
